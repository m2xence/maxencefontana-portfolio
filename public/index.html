About me

I’m a mechanical engineer working in international business development. At Business France in Los Angeles, I help French aerospace and space companies understand the North American market, identify potential partners, and prepare for commercial conversations.

Before moving to Los Angeles, I worked at Airbus Helicopters during my engineering apprenticeship and spent a summer with Messer Americas in Toronto. My work has ranged from aircraft configuration and engineering tools to organizing programs that bring companies and industry teams together.
